Assumptions & limits

Fixed-rate illustrations, not predictions or a probability of success. Both adults are assumed alive through the younger person’s age {{ plan_end_age }}. Income and contributions stop at retirement. Rates and locations stay fixed. No Social Security benefits, pensions, debt, loan payments, required minimum distributions, early-withdrawal penalties or variable market returns are modeled.

Household withdrawals use the selected percentage of combined opening investments, split equally between retirement and taxable pools, with fallback if one pool is insufficient. Pre-tax withdrawals are taxable; Roth is assumed qualified. Extra expenses use remaining brokerage at year end, never additional retirement withdrawals. Unfunded expenses are reported without carrying debt forward. Brokerage gains/dividend taxes and a purchase’s resale value are not modeled.

Growth applies to opening balances; cash flows occur at year end. Recurring spending and expense amounts increase with inflation. The first projection period is a full year. Saved plans retain their original starting year and spending snapshot until explicitly refreshed; formulas use the current app version.

Uses the existing 2026 federal/WA/OR tax estimates and indexed assumptions, including employee contribution limits. Excludes catch-up contributions, employer matches, many deductions/credits and local taxes. This is a scenario-planning tool, not a tax return or a complete financial-advice system.
