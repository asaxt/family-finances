What this preview assumes

Annual investment growth applies to the opening balance. Savings, income, and spending happen at year end and increase with inflation. Savings stop at retirement; retirement spending starts then. A goal at age 70 is paid during the year from age 70 to 71. “Plan through age 95” ends at your 95th birthday.

Balances cannot go below zero. Unfunded spending is shown separately and is not carried forward as debt. Retirement income above spending stays invested. Taxes, withdrawal rules, account-specific returns, benefits eligibility, and debt amortization are not calculated. Pre-tax and post-tax accounts are combined; include an estimated tax allowance in spending.

These are fixed-assumption illustrations, not predictions or a probability of success. Market volatility and return timing are not simulated. This is an investment projection, not a complete net-worth or financial plan.
