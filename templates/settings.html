Local data protection

How this installation protects financial information

✓Encrypted at rest
The database, Plaid secret, access tokens, transactions, and savings data are stored in one authenticated encrypted vault.
⌁Unlocked by your app password
The password decrypts a key that exists in memory only while the app is unlocked.
⌂Localhost only
The app remains limited to this computer at 127.0.0.1.

Keep full-disk encryption and encrypted backups enabled. Encryption cannot protect data from malicious software already running as your signed-in computer account while the app is unlocked.
